Kastrel Wallet is a self-custody wallet for USDT on the TRON network (TRC-20). Your recovery phrase and keys are created on your iPhone or iPad and stay there. There is no account to open, no sign-up and nobody holding your funds for you.

YOUR KEYS, ON YOUR DEVICE
- Create a new wallet in a few taps, or restore one with a 12- or 24-word recovery phrase
- The recovery phrase is kept in the device Keychain and is never sent to any server
- A 6-digit PIN and Face ID lock the app
- Simnetiq Ltd never sees or holds your funds and cannot move them, freeze them or recover them

SEND WITH CARE
- Paste an address or scan a QR code
- A review screen shows the recipient, the amount and the network fee before you confirm
- Clear guidance on TRX network fees: every USDT transfer on TRON burns energy, paid in TRX from your wallet
- Live status after sending, with the transaction ID and a link to Tronscan

RECEIVE IN SECONDS
- Your TRON address as a QR code, ready to share
- Request an exact amount in USDT or TRX
- Kastrel watches for incoming payments while the screen is open
- Receiving is free

UNDERSTAND YOUR ACTIVITY
- Full USDT history, cached on your device
- Balance and flow charts for 7 days up to all time, with drag-to-scrub
- Insights: money in, money out, net, fees paid, largest transfers
- Top counterparties, search and filters by amount, date and address

USDT AND TRX AT A GLANCE
- Portfolio value with 24-hour change
- Price charts, market stats and your position for each coin
- How many transfers your TRX balance can pay for

WIDGETS
- Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets show your USDT balance without opening the app

GOOD TO KNOW
- Kastrel Wallet supports USDT (TRC-20) and TRX on TRON only. USDT sent on another network, such as Ethereum or BNB Chain, will be lost
- Sending USDT needs a small TRX balance for network fees
- Transfers on the blockchain cannot be reversed. Check the address before you send
- Keep your recovery phrase offline. It is the only way to restore your wallet, and nobody can recover it for you
- Kastrel Wallet is a wallet only. It does not buy or sell crypto, offer cards, hold funds or provide investment advice
