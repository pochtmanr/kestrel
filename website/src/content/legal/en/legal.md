## Regulatory status

{{company}} publishes software. {{appName}} is a self-custody wallet: users create and keep their own keys on their own devices, and sign their own transactions.

{{company}}:

- does not hold or control users' cryptoassets or keys, and is not a custodian wallet provider;
- does not exchange cryptoassets for money or for other cryptoassets;
- does not transmit funds on behalf of users;
- is **not authorised or regulated by the Financial Conduct Authority**, and is not registered with it as a cryptoasset business, because it does not carry on activities that require this.

Nothing on this website or in the app is an invitation or inducement to buy, sell or invest in cryptoassets, and nothing is financial advice. Cryptoassets are high-risk. You can lose all the money you put in, and you are unlikely to be protected if something goes wrong. See the [risk disclosure](/risks/).

## Fees

The app is free. {{company}} currently charges no fee or commission on transfers. Network fees are paid by the user to the TRON network.

## Requests from authorities

Because the wallet is self-custody, we have no access to users' keys or funds. We cannot freeze, seize, move or recover cryptoassets, and we hold no account, identity or transaction records for users. Law enforcement and regulatory requests can be sent to [{{supportEmail}}](mailto:{{supportEmail}}). We respond to valid legal requests with whatever information we actually hold.
