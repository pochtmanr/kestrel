{{appName}} is not offered in every country. This page is part of our [terms of use](/terms/).

## Territories

You may not use the app if you are located in, resident in, or organised under the laws of:

- Cuba
- Iran
- North Korea
- Syria
- the Crimea, Donetsk and Luhansk regions
- mainland China

We may also choose not to make the app available in the App Store of other countries.

## Sanctioned persons

You may not use the app if you are:

- named on a sanctions list kept by the United Kingdom, the European Union, the United States or the United Nations;
- owned or controlled by someone who is; or
- acting on behalf of someone who is.

## Your own country's rules

Even where the app is available, holding or transferring cryptoassets may be restricted by local law. It is your responsibility to check that you are allowed to use the app where you live.

## No workarounds

You must not use a VPN or any other method to hide your location in order to use the app from a restricted territory.

## Changes

This list changes as sanctions and local laws change. Check it from time to time. The date at the top of the page shows when it was last updated.
