Email us at **[{{supportEmail}}](mailto:{{supportEmail}})**. We answer in English, Russian and Chinese, usually within two working days.

> **We will never ask for your recovery phrase.**
> Not by email, not in a chat, not on social media. Anyone who asks for your recovery phrase, your PIN or a "test transfer" is trying to steal your funds. This email address is our only support channel.

## What to include

- What you were trying to do and what happened
- Your iPhone or iPad model, and the iOS and app version
- For a problem with a transfer: the transaction ID (you can copy it from the transfer's details)

Do not send your recovery phrase or private keys. A wallet address or a transaction ID is safe to share.

## What we can help with

- How the app works
- A transfer that looks stuck or is not showing
- Network fees and TRX
- Faults in the app

## What we cannot do

Because {{brand}} is a self-custody wallet, we do not hold your keys or funds. That means we cannot:

- recover or reset a recovery phrase or PIN;
- cancel, reverse or refund a transfer;
- return funds sent to a wrong address or on a wrong network;
- unfreeze USDT frozen by its issuer.

## Common questions

**My transfer has not arrived.**
Look up the transaction ID on Tronscan. If it shows as confirmed, the funds are at the receiving address. If the recipient is an exchange, ask the exchange.

**I cannot send USDT.**
You need TRX in the same wallet to pay the TRON network fee. Send some TRX to your address and try again.

**I forgot my PIN.**
Erase the wallet in the app and restore it with your recovery phrase, then choose a new PIN.

**I lost my phone.**
Install the app on a new device and restore the wallet with your recovery phrase. Without the phrase, the wallet cannot be restored.

**I sent USDT on the wrong network.**
We cannot retrieve it. If it was sent to an exchange, the exchange may be able to help.

## Deleting your data

To remove everything the app stores, erase the wallet in Settings, then delete the app. To ask us to delete emails you sent us, write to [{{privacyEmail}}](mailto:{{privacyEmail}}). See the [privacy policy](/privacy/) for details.
